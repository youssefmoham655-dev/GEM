Welcome to Grand Egyptian museum

The Grand Egyptian Museum is a museum dedicated to ancient Egyptian civilization

It is located near the Pyramids of Giza

The museum preserves and displays a large collection of Egyptian artifacts

It gives visitors an opportunity to learn about Egypt's history and culture

The museum combines ancient Egyptian heritage with modern architecture

King Ramses II greets you at the door: Before you have a chance to see any of the wonders inside, you’re met by a 35-foot-tall statue of Ramses II, weighing some 83 tons. Transported from its former home in central Cairo in 2006, where it rested since 1954, the statue now stands in the museum’s atrium, surrounded by reflecting pools and illuminated by filtered daylight.

it is the largest archaeological museum in the world dedicated to a single civilization, housing over 100,000 ancient artifacts

Tutankhamun's Treasures: The museum includes integrated exhibition halls that display all of King Tutankhamun's golden possessions for the first time combined in one place.

Global space and size: The museum extends over an area of half a million square metres, and is characterized by an architectural design that combines Pharaonic civilization and modern technology, and is very close to the Pyramids of Giza.

Visual and cultural experience: Enjoy taking stunning photos next to the colossal statues and the great staircase facing the pyramids.
